03 — the safety shelf

Stay One Step Ahead of Financial Scams

Digital payments are convenient, but protecting your financial information is essential.

!   If in doubt, stop and verify
◇

Never share your UPI PIN

Your UPI PIN authorizes money leaving your account. Enter it only on your own payment screen.

○

Never share OTPs with callers

One-time passwords are yours alone. No legitimate person needs you to read one aloud.

□

Never share banking passwords

Use a different, hard-to-guess password for important accounts. Keep it private.

!

Verify suspicious payment requests

Check the recipient, amount, and reason before approving a request.

✓

Contact your bank through official channels

Use your bank’s official app, website, or phone number when you need help.
